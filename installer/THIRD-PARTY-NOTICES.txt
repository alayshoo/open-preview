Lumen bundles the following third-party software.

libmpv (libmpv-2.dll)
  The mpv media player library, built by the mpv-winbuild-cmake project
  (https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake). It statically includes
  FFmpeg, libass, libplacebo and other libraries.
